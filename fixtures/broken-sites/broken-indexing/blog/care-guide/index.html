How to care for your ceramic mugs

By the Example Store Ceramics team · Published 12 August 2026 · Updated 2 September 2026

Handmade stoneware is tough, but a few simple habits will keep your mugs looking new for years. Here is what we tell every customer who visits the studio.

Washing

All our mugs are dishwasher safe. For the longest-lasting glaze, wash them by hand with warm water and a soft sponge. Avoid steel wool and abrasive powders, which can scratch satin glazes.

Removing tea and coffee stains

Make a paste of baking soda and a little water, rub it gently on the stain with a soft cloth, and rinse. For stubborn stains, soak the mug for an hour in warm water with a spoon of baking soda.

Avoiding thermal shock

Sudden temperature changes can crack any ceramic. Do not pour boiling water into a mug straight from the fridge, and do not put a hot mug into cold water. Let it reach room temperature first.

Microwave use

Our mugs are microwave safe because our glazes contain no metal. Mugs with gold or silver lustre from other makers are not microwave safe.

Storing your mugs

Store mugs upright or hang them by the handle on a sturdy hook. Avoid stacking mugs inside each other, which can chip the rims.
