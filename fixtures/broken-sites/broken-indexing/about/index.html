About Example Store Ceramics

Our story

Example Store Ceramics started in 2019 when our founder, a potter trained in Kandy, opened a one-room studio inside the walls of Galle Fort. Today a team of four makes about 300 mugs a month using local clay, a gas kiln and glazes we mix ourselves.

How we make each mug

Each mug is thrown on a potter's wheel, left to dry for two days, trimmed, and fitted with a hand-pulled handle. It is bisque fired, glazed by dipping, and fired again to 1,240 °C. The high firing temperature makes the stoneware strong, non-porous and safe for daily use.

Our values

  • Small batches: we would rather make fewer mugs well than many quickly.
  • Honest materials: food-safe, lead-free glazes and local clay.
  • Low waste: clay trimmings are reclaimed and our packaging is recycled paper.
