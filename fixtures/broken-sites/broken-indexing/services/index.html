Custom mugs and pottery workshops

Custom handmade mugs

We make custom mugs for cafés, hotels, offices and weddings. Choose from our glaze colours or send us your own colour reference, and we can stamp your logo into the clay. The minimum order is 12 mugs and most orders are ready in four to six weeks.

How a custom order works

  1. Tell us the quantity, size, colour and any logo using the contact form.
  2. We send a quote and a photo of a sample mug within five working days.
  3. After you approve the sample, we make, fire and pack the full order.

Pottery workshops

Our three-hour beginner workshop runs every Saturday morning for up to six people. You will learn to centre clay and throw a mug on the wheel. We trim, glaze and fire your mug, and it is ready to collect or ship three weeks later. No experience is needed and all materials and aprons are included.

See prices for both services on the pricing page.
