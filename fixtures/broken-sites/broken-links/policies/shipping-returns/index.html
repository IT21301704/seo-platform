Shipping and returns policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026.

Shipping

We deliver anywhere in Sri Lanka by courier. Orders are packed within two working days and usually arrive two to four working days later. Delivery costs LKR 750 per order and is free for orders over LKR 15,000. You will receive a tracking number by email when your order leaves the studio. You can also collect your order from our studio in Galle Fort at no cost.

Returns

You can return unused mugs in their original packaging within 14 days of delivery. Email us at hello@example-store.com with your order number and we will send return instructions. We refund the full price of the returned items within five working days after we receive them. Return delivery costs are paid by the customer unless the item was faulty.

Damaged items

If an item arrives broken or chipped, send a photo to hello@example-store.com within 48 hours of delivery. We will send a free replacement or give you a full refund, whichever you prefer.

Custom orders

Custom mugs are made for you and cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or differ from the approved sample.
