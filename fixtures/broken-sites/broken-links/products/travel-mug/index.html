Ceramic travel mug

LKR 5,200

Our travel mug has straight sides, no handle and a natural cork lid, so it fits most car cup holders. The unglazed foot gives a steady grip, and the thick wall keeps tea warm for your commute.

Details

  • Material: high-fired stoneware

Delivery and returns

Delivered anywhere in Sri Lanka in two to four working days. Unused items can be returned within 14 days. See our shipping and returns policy.

Your mug is dishwasher and microwave safe. Avoid sudden temperature changes, such as pouring boiling water into a mug straight from the fridge. Read the full mug care guide.
