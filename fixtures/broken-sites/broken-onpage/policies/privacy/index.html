Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026.

What we collect

When you place an order, book a workshop or send us a message, we collect your name, email address, phone number and delivery address. We do not store payment card details; payments are handled by our payment provider.

How we use your data

We use your data only to process and deliver orders, manage workshop bookings and reply to your messages. We never sell your data. We send marketing emails only if you opt in, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for seven years for tax purposes. Messages are deleted after two years.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal data at any time by emailing hello@example-store.com. We follow the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act.
