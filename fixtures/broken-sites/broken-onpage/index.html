Handmade ceramic mugs from our studio in Galle

Example Store Ceramics is a small pottery studio inside Galle Fort, Sri Lanka. Every mug is thrown on the wheel, trimmed, glazed and fired by hand in small batches, so no two pieces are exactly alike.

Shop all mugs

What we make

We make stoneware mugs for coffee and tea, sold one at a time or as gift sets. Our glazes are food safe and lead free, and every mug is dishwasher and microwave safe.

  • Blue ceramic mug: our best-selling 350 ml mug in a deep ocean blue glaze.
  • Speckled stoneware mug: a sandy speckled clay body with a clear satin glaze.
  • Mug gift set: two mugs of your choice in a recycled paper gift box.

Custom orders and workshops

Cafés, hotels and families order custom mugs with their own colours or a stamped logo. You can also learn to throw your own mug at a three-hour pottery workshop in our studio. See our services and pricing.

Why people choose us

We have been making ceramics in Galle since 2019. We ship across Sri Lanka, answer every message within one working day, and accept returns within 14 days. Read our story, the frequently asked questions or our mug care guide.
